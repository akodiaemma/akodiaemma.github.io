AFRICAN PRODUCT ORIGINS

African produce. Fresh and dried, clearly described.

Give every product a clear identity: the crop, the origin, the variety and whether it is fresh or dried. Buyers need a practical description of what can be supplied.

191,508 tonnesUganda’s fruit and vegetable exports · FY 2024/25

The production and trade picture

Uganda’s Ministry of Agriculture reports fruit and vegetable exports of 191,508 tonnes for FY 2024/25. This is an export category total; it is not a production figure or a separate total for dried produce.

Fresh produce profiles can describe harvest stage, grading, packing and delivery timing. Dried produce profiles can describe the drying method, moisture, ingredients, storage and batch identity. Keep the two product forms distinct.

A clearer buyer conversation

Useful information to discuss and organise:

  • Crop, variety, growing origin and harvest period
  • Fresh: grading, packing and handling information
  • Dried: processing method, moisture and batch records

Crossgates brings supplier information and supporting documents together in a Verified Trade Passport so the business behind the product is easier to assess.
