Understanding verification

Know exactly what has been checked.

A Trade Passport brings information and evidence together. Each review conclusion needs a clear scope, a supporting source and visible limits.

Follow one example check

Source. Check. Conclusion.

Fictional review example
Supplier-provided source

A registration document is supplied.

In this fictional example, the reviewer receives a document from the supplier. The issuing registry has not been contacted.

What this does not establish

Receiving a document does not establish its authenticity or current validity.

Read the record behind the status.

When discussing an assessment, agree its scope and ask to see these details for the conclusions you rely on.

What was checked?
The specific claim, field or document, and the question the check addressed.
Against which source?
For example, a supplier-provided document or an issuing body’s record. The source matters.
Who checked it, and when?
The responsible reviewer, their role and relevant expertise, and the review date.
What remains open?
Missing, expired or conflicting information, the limits of the check and the next action.
Provided, reviewed and verified: what is the difference?

Provided: information or a document has been submitted. This alone does not establish that it is accurate or authentic.

Reviewed: a stated check has been completed. Checking that names match across documents is a limited consistency check.

Verified against a source: a particular fact has been confirmed against an identified source. Read which fact and source, who performed the check, and when. This does not extend to every claim about the supplier.

Ask which of these applies to each item. The name “Verified Trade Passport” is not blanket approval of the business or its products.

How do I interpret gaps and status labels?

Available means supplied, with the review state still to be checked. Pending or Needs review means a check or question remains open.

Missing means requested evidence is absent. Expired means the stated validity has ended. Not applicable needs a reason tied to the assessment scope.

Use the evidence and review notes alongside the label. An available document is not automatically a verified document.

What does STIV do, and what needs human review?

STIV helps explain trade-readiness requirements, organise information and guide preparation. AI-generated guidance can be incomplete or wrong and needs checking against relevant sources.

A human reviewer is responsible for the stated assessment and its conclusions. Any registry confirmation, site visit, laboratory test or specialist check must be identified separately when it has actually been performed.

Crossgates does not replace official certificates, regulatory decisions or a buyer’s own due diligence.

Your questions, answered

Know what to expect.

Can I start if I am not yet ready to export?

Yes. Start with the information and documents you already have. Crossgates helps you identify gaps and understand the next steps for your business and target market. Contact the team to discuss current onboarding availability.

Does a Trade Passport certify my products or guarantee a sale?

No. It presents business information, supporting evidence and review status. It does not replace official certificates, regulatory approvals or a buyer’s own checks, and it does not guarantee a sale.

What does “reviewed” mean?

It means a stated check has been completed. Its scope may be limited to document consistency or completeness. It does not automatically establish authenticity or confirm every supplier claim. See how to read the source, reviewer, date and limits behind a status.

Can buyers browse every supplier’s documents?

No. Supplier profiles are private by default. Buyers see only profiles and information approved for buyer access. Contact Crossgates to discuss the information available for your assessment.

Can our organisation use Crossgates with a group of businesses?

Yes. Contact us to discuss your programme objectives, participating businesses, target markets and reporting needs. We can then agree an appropriate pilot scope and readiness process.

How do I get started, and what does it cost?

Contact the team to discuss your business, supplier-review needs or programme. We will confirm the available service, scope and pricing before you proceed. Existing users can log in to the platform.
